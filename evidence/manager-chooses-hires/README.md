  noise PM went; its place listed the law's own hire (a child of pod-1, the fittest), a child of
  hire-1 (the other fit pod) and a plain hire. **The manager picked the law's own hire (c1)** and
  briefed the new PM: "Inherit the pod-1 momentum rule with rebalance_every 5. Keep gross near 1.0,
  stay long-only, and manage risk carefully; fitness is measured net of thinking cost, so keep
  thinking brief." It never wrote a firm direction. Its thinking cost $0.027 over 31 wakes, the
  centre's; the whole run $0.590, every key under its limit.
