# A pitch is argued before its seed: the recorded real run

- `debate.cassette.json`: the evidence (cycle 70, 2026-10-22). A hosted made-up market with four
  edge sectors, a noise sector and news (a fraud headline on K03 dated 2024-09-26, out at its
  close), under a 2-day debate law. Three pods, alpha (K, its universe holds K03), beta (L) and
  gamma (T), with scripted PMs that pitch 20-day momentum on the first morning. Three real
  Haiku 5.5 centre minds, each on its own $0.25 key: a bull, a bear given headlines by the
  operator at registration, and the firm's manager. Five mornings from 26 September 2024, 16
  tool calls a wake, thinking charged every step. Made with the command
  `uv run python acceptance/pitch_debate.py --write-world DIR` prints.
- What happened: all three pitches passed the gates; their debates opened on 27 September. The
  bear argued against each (the numbers are "not believable", IC 0.37 to 0.57 and Sharpe 15 to
  27 for a momentum rule; for alpha also K03's fraud headline); the bull argued for each, granting
  the numbers look too good and asking for a smaller seed. The manager held all three the same
  morning: "far beyond any credible daily edge; treat as probable leakage or artifact", asking
  for a fresh window and results net of costs. Physics refused each seed the next morning with
  the manager's reason. Thinking: $0.018 in all.
- Two things the minds got wrong, both from what they were shown: they read the gates' numbers
  as before costs (the gates judge net of fees; the pitch view does not say so), and they
  judged a planted edge implausible because it is (the made-up market's edge is far stronger
  than any real one). Both are in the book.
- `uv run python acceptance/pitch_debate.py` judges it (part B: physics' answers to whatever the
  minds chose: each hold refused with its reason, no ruling late, twice or by anyone else, the
  reviewers wrote only in threads, no key past its limit) and replays it (IDENTICAL, $0). What
  physics does in each case (a seed paid early, silence paid at the close) is proven by parts A
  and B0.
